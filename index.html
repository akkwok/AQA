NVIDIA HGX™ Rubin NVL8,
Powering Every AI and HPC Workload

Paired with dual Intel® Xeon® 6776P processors and a fully modular, tool-less design, the ASUS compute tray translates this platform leap into lower cost per token, faster time-to-model, and a stronger return on every enterprise's AI Factory investment.

FAQs

  • How do I know which ASUS rack — XR R1I12B-M1BLFS or XR R1I12B-M1BHFS — fits my data center?
    How do I know which ASUS rack — XR R1I12B-M1BLFS or XR R1I12B-M1BHFS — fits my data center? It depends primarily on your data center's cooling infrastructure and rack power architecture. The 2U XA NR1I-E12LR is designed for full direct liquid cooling (DLC), while the 3U XA NR1I-E12L uses a hybrid-cooling architecture. Depending on the deployment environment, different CDU and power configurations, including in-row or in-rack CDU and busbar or PSU-based solutions, can be evaluated. ASUS can assess the customer's facility cooling and power infrastructure to recommend the appropriate rack configuration.
  • Does the HGX Rubin NVL8 platform require liquid cooling, or can it run in an existing air-cooled data center?
    Does the HGX Rubin NVL8 platform require liquid cooling, or can it run in an existing air-cooled data center? The HGX Rubin NVL8 platform requires liquid cooling for the GPUs. ASUS offers both full direct liquid cooling (DLC) and hybrid-cooling designs to support different data center environments. The 2U XA NR1I-E12LR is designed for full DLC, while the 3U XA NR1I-E12L combines liquid cooling for the GPUs with air cooling for other components. Existing data centers should be assessed for cooling capacity and facility readiness before deployment.
  • What are the benefits of liquid cooling compared to traditional air cooling?
    What are the benefits of liquid cooling compared to traditional air cooling? Liquid cooling provides more efficient heat removal for high-density GPU systems by transferring heat directly through the liquid cooling loop, reducing reliance on server-level air cooling. Actual efficiency improvements depend on data center conditions, cooling architecture, and operating environment.
  • What are the power and cooling requirements per rack for this platform?
    What are the power and cooling requirements per rack for this platform? A full L11 rack supports nine XA NR1I-E12LR compute trays with 72 NVIDIA Rubin GPUs and 18 Intel Xeon 6776P processors. The rack has a system power budget of approximately 216 kW and features a fully liquid-cooled architecture for both CPUs and GPUs. A 2+2 redundant 110 kW power shelf design and a 5000A rack busbar provide the required rack-level power infrastructure.
  • What deployment and after-sales support does ASUS provide for global rollouts of this platform?
    What deployment and after-sales support does ASUS provide for global rollouts of this platform? ASUS provides deployment planning and technical support for HGX Rubin NVL8 rack-scale solutions, including infrastructure assessment, rack integration, and system validation. Deployment and after-sales service options may vary by region and project requirements. ASUS teams can work with customers to define the appropriate service scope for each deployment.
